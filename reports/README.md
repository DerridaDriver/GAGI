# Public research archive

These are selected existing Local Lab reports and aggregate artifacts, copied **byte-for-byte**. Originals remain outside this Git repository and were not edited. The source manifest records SHA-256 and logical provenance without a machine-specific root. `.gitattributes` disables text normalization for the snapshots so a clone retains their original bytes.

## Read old reports in context

- Reports are historical observations, not current recommendations. Some recommend interventions later rejected, or report pending stages that later completed.
- Wave 1's original positive labels were later kept diagnostic-only; 0.8A records `do_not_promote=true`.
- Anchor re-review and Pairwise Recovery are MEMORY_CONTAMINATED and AUXILIARY / RESEARCH ONLY. The original reports' reliability language is not silently rewritten.
- The 0.9C record preserves the original 26-versus-27 discrepancy. The durable count is 27; the evaluator later confirmed the spoken 26 was a slip.
- 0.9D-A has 4 reviewed setups, not 24 completed reviews. The 20 unreviewed setups remain missing, not negative.
- 0.9F's statistical INCONCLUSIVE and engineering REJECTED_FOR_CONTINUATION are separate preserved decisions.
- The evaluator's publication-time clarification interprets 0.9G TARGET_HIT as WORTH_LEARNING. Raw historical field names and judgments remain unchanged. It is not a laughter measurement.
- Older cross-run statistical tests and mechanistic claims remain as archival text. They do not establish randomized causal effects or universal laws.

## Scope

Only the listed reports/aggregates are included. Raw provider requests/responses, full reference corpora, private databases, machine paths, training checkpoints and runtime caches are excluded. Some snapshots mention private supporting artifacts by filename; those mentions are not claims that the files are public. No fictional substitute files were created.

The snapshots include experiment text and some generated joke examples, including morbid or socially uncomfortable material. These are research artifacts, not factual claims about people or groups.

For the current synthesis and methodological qualifications, read the [root README](../README.md). For release checks, see the [publication audit](PUBLICATION_AUDIT.md).
